# Daqin identity

The emblem draws on a carved Qin seal. Converging paths and a central mark
represent unified coordination and an accountable handoff. Vermilion and ivory
keep it recognizable on light and dark backgrounds.

`daqin-mark.png` is the original generated raster master, with transparency
outside the seal. It was created with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool on
2026-10-02. The emblem contains no lettering and is retained unchanged for Daqin.
It is an original project asset, not a reproduction of a historical inscription
or a third-party brand. No claim of trademark clearance is made. The project
MIT License covers this asset to the extent rights apply.

## Design brief

A compact modern seal inspired by Qin unification: independent paths converge
around one central impression. Bold geometry, a vermilion seal and warm ivory
paths express a shared coordination standard and a trusted handoff.
